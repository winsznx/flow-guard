---
title: "Create a Bounty"
description: "Lock a prize for a piece of work and fund it from your wallet."
icon: "trophy"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the work">
    Go to **Bounties → Create a bounty**. Add a title and explain how to take part: what to do, how to send you the work and how you'll pick winners. People send their work to you outside FlowGuard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the prize">
    Pick BCH or a token, then set the prize per winner and the number of winners. To pay in a token, use the wallet with permission to create that token, usually the wallet that created it. The prize per winner can't change later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the dates">
    You can pay winners from the start date. The end date is optional. After it you can't pay winners, but you can still cancel and get the rest back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and fund">
    Choose **Create bounty**. The app calls `POST /api/bounties/create`, then `GET /api/bounties/:id/funding-info` returns the funding `wcTransaction`. Approve it in your wallet, and `POST /api/bounties/:id/confirm-funding` marks the bounty `ACTIVE`. Your wallet may need one setup step first, so you might approve two requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Funding locks the whole prize, plus a little BCH the contract uses for later network fees. See [Pay a Winner](/guides/bounties/claim) for the payout flow.
