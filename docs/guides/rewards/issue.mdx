description: "How the program creator sends rewards, and what people receive."

---

Rewards arrive in people's wallets. There's nothing for them to claim.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the program">
    Open the reward program with the wallet that created it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a reward">
    Enter the person's address and the amount, up to the limit per reward, and choose **Send**. The app calls `POST /api/rewards/:id/distribute`, FlowGuard's server approves it, and you approve it in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    `POST /api/rewards/:id/confirm-distribute` records the reward. The network fee comes from the contract.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Under **Manage** you can pause the program so no rewards can be sent, resume it, or cancel it to get back what hasn't been sent.

## Reward vs. Stream vs. Airdrop

| Product | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Reward | Different amounts to people you pick, one at a time |
| Stream | One person, paid bit by bit over time |
| Airdrop | The same amount to many people, who each claim it |
