---
title: "Create a Grant"
description: "Lock a grant up front, split into milestones, and fund it from your wallet."
icon: "chart-line"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Who and how much">
    Go to **Grants → Create a grant**. Name the grant, paste the recipient's address and pick BCH or a token. To pay in a token, use the wallet with permission to create that token. Set the amount per milestone, which can't change later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Milestones">
    Add a name and note for each milestone. FlowGuard keeps these, and the contract only counts how many milestones have been released.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rules">
    Choose whether you can pause or cancel later, and whether the recipient can move the rest of the grant to a new address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and fund">
    The app calls `POST /api/grants/create`, then `GET /api/grants/:id/funding-info` returns the funding `wcTransaction`. Approve it in your wallet, and `POST /api/grants/:id/confirm-funding` marks the grant `ACTIVE`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Funding locks every milestone at once in a `GrantCovenant`. See [Grant Milestones](/guides/grants/milestones) for releasing them.
